About Us

Dedicated to delivering free, quality healthcare for all.

We are an Awesome Team

Touch Hospital thrives on compassion and care. With a unified mission, we work together to ensure no life is left untreated due to financial constraints.

Founded on the belief that healthcare is a right, not a privilege, Touch Hospital works tirelessly to bring quality medical aid to underprivileged communities—free of charge. Our doctors, surgeons, and volunteers collaborate to serve the underserved with empathy and integrity.

With every procedure, check-up, and consultation, we aim to not only heal but also instill hope. Our team is our backbone—and every member carries this vision with pride.
